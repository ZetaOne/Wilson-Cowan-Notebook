Study kit

Scripts, XPP files and notebooks for working through the paper yourself.

  • one_population.odeOne excitatory population: bistable for inputs between −1.8 and 1.8.
  • wc_oscillator.odeThe Wilson–Cowan pair with the parameters of their Figure 11: a limit cycle.
  • wc_hysteresis.odeTheir Figure 4/5 parameters: three steady states and hysteresis in p.
  • stm_form.odeThe same network written for the inputs (the STM form).
  • amari_bump.odeAmari's field on a ring of 240 points with a Mexican-hat kernel (XPP's conv): a bump that outlives its stimulus.
  • theta_network.ode200 θ-neurons with Lorentzian excitabilities and shared synapses.
  • mpr_mean_field.odeThe exact Montbrió–Pazó–Roxin mean field.
  • langevin_population.odeLangevin approximation of a finite stochastic population: noise-driven switching.
  • 01_one_population.ipynbCrossings with the diagonal, the S-curve, hysteresis by ramping, folds with AUTO.
  • 02_wc_phase_plane.ipynbNullclines, three steady states, the limit cycle, the Hopf point and frequency against input with AUTO.
  • 03_neural_fields.ipynbAmari's bump condition and a bump that remembers where the stimulus was.
  • 04_spiking_mean_field.ipynbA θ-neuron raster and rate, and the exact MPR mean field: volleys versus smooth responses.
  • 05_finite_size.ipynbGillespie simulations, 1/N fluctuations and noise-driven switching.

Wilson-Cowan-Notebook, a guided reading of C. C. Chow and Y. Karimipanah, Before and beyond the Wilson–Cowan equations, J. Neurophysiol. 123 (2020) 1645–1656 (the paper). Every simulation is computed by Bard Ermentrout's XPPAUT through pyauto-xpp.
